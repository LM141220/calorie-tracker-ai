# Post-V1 technical backlog

This backlog is intentionally separate from the V1 account-approval controls.
Effort estimates are relative and assume one maintainer.

| Area          | Task                                                                                                                                     | Priority | Effort | Impact                                                                 | Risk if delayed                                                             | Recommended timing                                                           |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Security      | Review RLS policies, storage policies, and `SECURITY DEFINER` functions quarterly; include negative tests for pending/rejected accounts. | High     | Medium | Detects authorization regressions in the primary data boundary.        | A policy or function change could expose or mutate another account's data.  | First review before public launch; then every 3 months.                      |
| Security      | Rotate Supabase, Gemini/OpenRouter, and Resend secrets; document an owner-only emergency rotation procedure.                             | Medium   | Low    | Limits the lifetime of accidentally exposed credentials.               | A leaked secret may remain usable until manually revoked.                   | Establish before launch; review every 6 months and after suspected exposure. |
| Security      | Monitor authentication failures, repeated signup attempts, and unexpected account-status transitions.                                    | Medium   | Medium | Makes account abuse and auth regressions visible.                      | Abuse may go unnoticed; noisy signup email is possible.                     | Within the first month after launch.                                         |
| Security      | Add rate limiting and abuse controls to signup and AI analysis endpoints.                                                                | High     | Medium | Reduces automated signup and provider-cost abuse.                      | Resource or AI-provider cost spikes.                                        | Before opening signup broadly.                                               |
| Dependencies  | Enable Dependabot or equivalent weekly npm advisory and update PRs; review and merge deliberately.                                       | Medium   | Low    | Shortens exposure to known dependency issues.                          | Vulnerable transitive dependencies can accumulate.                          | Within 2 weeks after V1.                                                     |
| Dependencies  | Re-evaluate `@ducanh2912/next-pwa` maintenance and compatibility; prototype a migration to Serwist separately.                           | Medium   | Medium | Keeps service-worker tooling maintainable and compatible with Next.js. | Future framework upgrades may become blocked or cache behavior may diverge. | Before the next major Next.js upgrade.                                       |
| Dependencies  | Perform a monthly dependency update pass with lockfile review, focused tests, and `npm audit`.                                           | Medium   | Low    | Prevents large, risky upgrade batches.                                 | Upgrade debt and security patch delays grow.                                | Monthly.                                                                     |
| Observability | Add production runtime error monitoring with source maps and alert routing.                                                              | High     | Low    | Speeds diagnosis of user-facing failures.                              | Errors may only be discovered through user reports.                         | Before or immediately after launch.                                          |
| Observability | Track authentication outcomes and approval flow failures without recording passwords, tokens, or unnecessary personal data.              | Medium   | Medium | Highlights email/OAuth, callback, and approval regressions.            | Failed sign-ins or stuck pending users may be hard to diagnose.             | Within the first month after launch.                                         |
| Observability | Add structured API error metrics and alerts for AI provider failures, latency, and quota exhaustion.                                     | Medium   | Medium | Makes reliability and provider cost issues actionable.                 | AI failures or provider limits can silently degrade meal entry.             | Within the first month after launch.                                         |
